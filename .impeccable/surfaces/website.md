# Surface brief: openregulations.ai website

Scope: home (Persuade), /dockets (Operate), /docket/:id (Read), /about (Read).
Audience and job: see PRODUCT.md. Constraint: the owner pinned a "tech + policy" feel; the
roll (`concept-seed`) did not run in this session, so the direction below was derived by hand.

## Direction contract

THESIS: AI analysis of public comments on federal rules, said plainly. Owner feedback: the first pass
(copies-vs-voices diff, toggle, section numbering) was too detailed; the site leads with the analysis itself.
Refuses the navy gov-portal hero with card grid, and the dark terminal dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Tech + policy. Cool white ground (#F5F6F8), ink #0A0E1A, one committed ultramarine (#2433F0)
owning the home field and primary actions, highlighter yellow (#FFE14A) only for the form-letter share.
Public Sans (U.S. federal design-system face) heavy for display; JetBrains Mono for agencies, IDs, counts.
Square corners. Hairline line figures (MIT) on the hero and in empty states.

STORY: Visitors see what the site does in one line, pick an analysis, and read it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: ultramarine field, headline "AI analysis of public comments on federal rules.",
one sentence, one action "Browse analyses", a Hairline Sieve figure that plays until the pointer arrives.
Docket page: title, comment count, actions, then the analysis with a small stats card and contents.

FORM: Plain editorial list + document, first on the ordered list. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
